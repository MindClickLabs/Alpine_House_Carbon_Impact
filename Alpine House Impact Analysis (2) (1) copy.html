The story for the
Jackson Hole community
🏔️
Living in Jackson Hole comes with a shared responsibility. We are caretakers of one of the most breathtaking, fragile ecosystems on earth. That's why we're incredibly proud to share that Alpine House has officially saved 921.96 kg of greenhouse gases (CO₂e), verified by EPA standards.
To our local community, we know what matters most isn't just the data — it's the impact on our backyard. This carbon reduction is the equivalent of taking nearly 100 round-trip commuter drives over Teton Pass off the road. It keeps our mountain air clear, protects the cold winter temperatures our snowpack depends on, and has the same environmental power as growing a small grove of 15 trees.
